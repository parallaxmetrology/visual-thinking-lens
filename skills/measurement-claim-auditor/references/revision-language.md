# Revision language

## Downgrade verdicts

| instead of | use |
|---|---|
| "this image is better" | "this image sits at <position> under <metric>" |
| "the model understands composition" | "outputs vary along <axis> with the prompt (rigidity ratio r over n prompts)" |
| "this proves collapse" | "consistent with a collapse candidate on <axis>; needs replication across a comparable set" |
| "the prompt was obeyed" | "measured structure moved in the prompt-intended direction" |
| "painters avoid the center" | "in this set of reproductions, n% sit within 0.15 of center, reached mainly by <balance / one body>" |

## Scope markers

"In this image…" · "Within this comparable set (n = …)…" · "Under kernel canon3-v8, diffusion off…" · "Measured on one machine…" · "In these reproductions…" · "Excluding n QA failures…"

## Evidence markers

"supported by a narrow Δx window (0.09 vs 0.13)" · "supported by low μ with high SDI" · "supported by r_v with a high tail_gap" · "rigidity ratio 1.2 across 30 prompts"

## Boundary markers

"This is not an aesthetic ranking." · "This doesn't establish intent." · "This doesn't measure meaning or subject." · "Local until replicated." · "Describes the reproduction, not the object."

## Clean templates

**One image:** "This image's structural mass sits <where>, <how gathered or spread>, measured on <source/resolution>. The reading is local and says nothing about quality or about the maker's defaults."

**Set:** "Across n images from <source, selection>, <metric> occupies a window of <w> (median <m>), against <w2> for <comparison set>. The difference is consistent with <pattern>; the sets differ in <confounds>."
