Where does the weight go?

Drop in a handful of images: one engine's outputs, a series of your paintings, ten photos of anything. The lens finds the structural mass in each and plots where it lands. One centered image is a choice. Twenty centered images about twenty different things are a default.

Drop images here, or choose files. Everything runs on your machine; nothing is uploaded.

Mass centroids in the frame

Each dot is one image's centre of structural mass. Cross = frame centre. Ring = 0.15 from centre. Hover a dot or a card to link them.

The set, as a fingerprint

metricp10medianp90window

Window = p90 − p10, the range the set actually uses on each axis. Narrow windows across varied subjects point to a learned default; wide windows point to choice.

Δx · Δy · placement

Where the centroid of structural mass sits, from −0.5 to 0.5. Negative Δy is the upper frame.

SDI · dispersion

How far mass spreads from its own centre: about 0.1 for a tight blob, 0.4 when spread to the edges.

μ · cohesion

One dominant body (→1) or many equal fragments (→0). Two equal halves read low by design.

xₚ · peripheral pull

The share of edge energy in the outer 15% of the frame. Low means everything is pulled inward.

rᵥ · void

How much of the frame carries almost no structure. Quiet ground versus edge activity everywhere.

Gold = what was measured

The mask keeps mid-strength gradients (85th–97th percentile): texture, marks, modelling. Crisp flat shapes on blank grounds can fool it, so check the gold.

Kernel canon3-v8, ported to JavaScript. Browsers resize images differently from OpenCV, so numbers here are close to, not identical with, the reference Python package (pip install git+https://github.com/parallaxmetrology/visual-thinking-lens).
